# Fuzzing in the gate

- Status: Approved (2026-10-13, approved by a human in the final review)
- Date: 2026-10-12
- Related: Sections 1 and 7 of [testing.md](../design/testing.md), requirement 7.2

## Background

Section 7 of testing.md says the gate does "replaying a fixed corpus and short fuzzing". On the other hand, section 1 says "gates are deterministic". Fuzzing tries different inputs on each run, so strictly speaking it is not deterministic.

## Decision

- `./gradlew build` (`scripts/check.sh`) runs both of the following.
  - The usual `test` task: replays the fixed corpus (`vtfx-core/src/test/resources/.../TerminalFuzzTestInputs/write/`). It is deterministic.
  - The `fuzz` task: fuzzes for 30 seconds with Jazzer. `check` depends on it.
- Whatever fuzzing finds, on whichever run, is a real bug. There are no false positives. Therefore a fuzzing failure is treated not as non-determinism of the gate but as a bug that had not been found yet. Jazzer saves the input found into the corpus directory, and from then on the deterministic `test` replays it.
- Hangs (10 seconds or more for one input) and memory exhaustion (a 1 GiB heap) are also detected as failures.
- Long fuzzing runs with `scripts/fuzz.sh <duration>`, and the results are appended to `build/reports/fuzzing-log.tsv`.
- The fixed corpus consists of hand-written seeds; the corpus generated by fuzzing (`.cifuzz-corpus/`, several MB) is not committed.

## Alternatives considered

- **Fix the random seed and the number of runs**: With Jazzer's JUnit integration, the only way to pass libFuzzer's `-seed` is an internal system property (`jazzer.internal.arg.*`). Also, fixing the seed means trying the same inputs every time, which makes putting short fuzzing in the gate less meaningful.
- **Remove fuzzing from the gate**: Not taken, because it would relax the rules of testing.md.

## Impact of reversing

Remove `tasks.named("check") { dependsOn(fuzz) }` from `vtfx.fuzz.gradle.kts` in `build-logic`.
